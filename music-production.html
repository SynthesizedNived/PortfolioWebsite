Music Production

Music production is the process of creating, recording, editing, arranging, and producing music using instruments, digital audio workstations, plugins, and other audio tools. It involves combining different elements such as melodies, chords, rhythms, vocals, sound effects, and instruments to create a complete musical composition. I use digital audio tools for experimenting with melodies, arranging music, creating beats, working with virtual instruments, and exploring different sounds and production techniques. Through working with music production, I have developed an understanding of basic recording, MIDI, audio editing, arrangement, mixing, effects, and working with digital instruments and plugins. It has also helped me understand how different musical elements can be combined and processed to create a balanced and finished track.
